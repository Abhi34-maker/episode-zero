    About this prototype

    The problem. A new writer often has a spark but does not know the genre, the structure or how to open episode one. Many stop before they draft anything.

    What this does. It interviews the writer, drafts a concept and outline, flags possible gaps, reviews the opening episode and sets a seven-day plan. The writer decides everything. It does not write the story.

    Hypothesis. Writers who reach a clear concept in one sitting are more likely to draft episode one within seven days. The measure would be the share of new writers who go from idea to first-episode draft in seven days. This has not been tested with real writers.

    Limits. The AI can be wrong or generic. Treat every suggestion as a prompt for your own judgement. The finished example is pre-written by the author, not live output.
